Common questions

The app finds few or no trips

With limited Photos access the app only sees the photos you chose, and a handful of photos rarely makes a trip. Allow all photos in the iPhone's Settings → AI Photo Book → Photos. The first look through a large library takes a few minutes; leave the app open while it works.

What exactly do I pay for?

Making and editing books is free. The purchase is the print files for one book: high-resolution PDFs of the pages and the cover. After buying, new files after changes to that book are free. Printing and delivery are ordered and paid on the printer's own website.

How do I order the printed book?

In the book, tap Print this book. Choose the printer and the country it is delivered to, follow How to order, and share the print files to the printer from the share sheet.

I bought print files but they are locked again

Purchases are kept in the app on your iPhone. If the app was deleted and installed again, its books are gone too. A book you paid for and then deleted leaves its purchase for your next book. If something still looks wrong, write to us with the date of the purchase from your App Store receipt.

I got a book with a code on the back

Scan the QR code, or redeem the code in the app: Settings → Redeem a code. The print files for your own first book are then free.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is worked out on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for the few things that do leave the phone.
